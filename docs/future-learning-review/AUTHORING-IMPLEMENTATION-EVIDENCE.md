## Implemented

- Existing lazy editorial guidance now explicitly supports optional independent prediction/explanation/variation with current content blocks, without a reveal schema, quiz, compulsory response or automatic progress. It distinguishes the current canonical reading from an immutable original manuscript when revising.
- Coverage guidance permits purposeful topic/alias reformulation when useful and makes a title-only miss an insufficient basis for creating another resource. It does not add a required multi-call ceremony during tutoring.
- Manuscript preview candidates explicitly identify their bounded title-derived literal query scope and include a compact eligible canonical excerpt. Candidate identity/revision/rendition remains exact; their wording is not a duplicate score or a complete semantic coverage review.
- Existing exact link, catalogue, membership and CAS validators already supply actionable structural failures. No additional semantic validator or authoring DSL was introduced. Input schema and editorial requirements are unchanged.

## Educational review and limits

The benchmark asks learners to explain which boundary failed instead of accepting any displayed output as success. It keeps API status, parse grammar and data contract separate, and offers a small discriminating change rather than a finished project. Optional prompts remain readable as ordinary explanation; no completion or inferred mastery is recorded. The local fixture confirms publication/reading creates no LearningDevelopments.

Primary MDN contracts were checked through Firecrawl: [JSON.parse](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/JSON/parse), [Number.isInteger](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/Number/isInteger) and [Response.ok](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Response/ok). All prose/examples are original; references are link-only. The safe-integer qualification was retained as a targeted clarification because integer representation does not guarantee exact arbitrary-size counts.

This is development evidence, not a study of learning gains or a fresh installed ChatGPT tutoring conversation. Rich rendering, source correctness, reuse and guarded revision support the capability; publication success alone does not certify the educational quality of future GPT material.
