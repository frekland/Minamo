# Community conduct

Minamo is a small learning-led open-source project. Contributions should make it easier for people to explore, learn and collaborate.

Please:

- Be respectful and patient with people of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code with concrete evidence; do not make criticism personal.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not share another person's records, messages or identifying information.
- Welcome questions and good-faith mistakes. Explain terminology when it helps.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, intimidation and unwanted personal contact.

Maintainers may moderate comments, close threads or decline contributions that breach these expectations. If an issue requires private handling, contact the maintainer through GitHub rather than posting identifying details publicly.

This project is volunteer-maintained; participation does not imply an obligation to provide support or immediate replies.
